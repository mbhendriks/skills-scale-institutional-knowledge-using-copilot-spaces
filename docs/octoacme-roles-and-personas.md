# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Tech Leads / Engineering Leads

### Role Summary
Tech Leads guide the technical approach and help engineering teams deliver maintainable, reliable solutions.

### Responsibilities
- Set technical direction and facilitate architecture decisions
- Break down complex work and identify technical risks and dependencies
- Support Developers through design and code reviews
- Coordinate engineering practices and share technical context

### Goals
- Keep implementation aligned with product needs and engineering standards
- Address technical risks early and enable sustainable delivery

### Typical Communication
- Technical designs, architecture decisions, and engineering planning
- Risk and dependency discussions with project and product leads

### Collaboration
Tech Leads work with Project Managers on estimates, dependencies, and delivery risks; with Product Managers on technical trade-offs; and with Developers on design and implementation. They coordinate with QA/Testing on testability and quality risks, and explain technical options and constraints to Stakeholders.

---

## Release Managers

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate the activities needed to prepare, approve, and deliver changes to production.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain release plans, milestones, and readiness criteria
- Coordinate deployment, rollback, and release communication plans
- Track release blockers and confirm required approvals
- Capture release outcomes and follow-up actions

### Goals
- Make releases predictable, safe, and transparent
- Ensure teams and customers are prepared for changes

### Typical Communication
- Release calendars, readiness reviews, and deployment updates
- Go/no-go decisions, release notes, and post-release follow-ups

### Collaboration
Release Managers coordinate timelines and blockers with Project Managers and confirm scope and launch expectations with Product Managers. They work with Developers on deployment and rollback plans, with QA/Testing on test completion and release sign-off, and with Stakeholders on readiness, timing, and release communications.

---

## Business Analysts

### Role Summary
Business Analysts clarify business needs and translate them into requirements the delivery team can understand and validate.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit and document business requirements and workflows
- Identify gaps, assumptions, and dependencies
- Help define clear, testable acceptance criteria
- Validate that proposed solutions address the stated needs

### Goals
- Reduce ambiguity between business needs and delivered solutions
- Ensure requirements are traceable and understandable

### Typical Communication
- Requirement specifications, process maps, and decision records
- Workshops and reviews with business and delivery teams

### Collaboration
Business Analysts help Project Managers clarify scope and dependencies and work with Product Managers to connect requirements to product priorities. They clarify requirements for Developers, partner with QA/Testing to make acceptance criteria verifiable, and gather context and confirm needs with Stakeholders.

---

## UX / Product Designers

### Role Summary
UX / Product Designers shape accessible, useful experiences through research, interaction design, and validation.

### Responsibilities
- Research user needs and map user journeys
- Create and validate flows, wireframes, and prototypes
- Apply usability and accessibility principles
- Document interaction patterns and design decisions

### Goals
- Make products usable, accessible, and aligned with user needs
- Validate experience assumptions before and during implementation

### Typical Communication
- Research findings, prototypes, and design specifications
- Design reviews and usability feedback

### Collaboration
UX / Product Designers align research and design work with Project Managers’ plans and Product Managers’ outcomes and priorities. They work with Developers to deliver feasible designs, with QA/Testing to validate usability and accessibility, and with Stakeholders to gather feedback and review experience decisions.

---

## Security / Compliance Reviewers

### Role Summary
Security / Compliance Reviewers identify security, privacy, and regulatory requirements and assess whether solutions meet them.

### Responsibilities
- Identify relevant security and compliance requirements
- Review designs and changes for risks and control gaps
- Recommend mitigations and track findings through resolution
- Advise on evidence and approvals needed for release

### Goals
- Reduce security, privacy, and compliance risk
- Make required controls and review decisions clear to the team

### Typical Communication
- Risk assessments, review findings, and mitigation tracking
- Readiness decisions and compliance evidence

### Collaboration
Security / Compliance Reviewers help Project Managers plan review milestones and track risks, and work with Product Managers to clarify product obligations. They advise Developers on mitigations, coordinate with QA/Testing on security and compliance validation, and communicate risks and review outcomes to Stakeholders.

---

## Support / Customer Success Representatives

### Role Summary
Support / Customer Success Representatives bring customer issues and operational readiness needs into planning and delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Share customer feedback, recurring issues, and support trends
- Prepare support guidance, training, and escalation paths for changes
- Help assess customer impact and readiness
- Report post-release issues and adoption feedback

### Goals
- Smooth customer transitions and effective issue resolution
- Ensure delivery teams account for real customer needs

### Typical Communication
- Support tickets, customer feedback summaries, and readiness materials
- Release briefings and post-release feedback

### Collaboration
Support / Customer Success Representatives coordinate customer impact and readiness with Project Managers and share feedback to inform Product Managers’ priorities. They provide Developers with actionable issue reports, work with QA/Testing to reproduce and verify fixes, and keep Stakeholders informed about customer concerns and support readiness.

---

## Data / Analytics Partners

### Role Summary
Data / Analytics Partners help teams define, collect, and interpret evidence about product performance and outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define measurable outcomes and analytics requirements
- Advise on instrumentation, data quality, and privacy-aware collection
- Analyze results and communicate limitations and trends
- Support reporting and post-launch evaluation

### Goals
- Make product decisions measurable and evidence-based
- Provide trustworthy insight into outcomes and usage

### Typical Communication
- Metric definitions, dashboards, and analysis summaries
- Instrumentation reviews and post-launch readouts

### Collaboration
Data / Analytics Partners help Project Managers establish progress reporting and partner with Product Managers to define success metrics. They coordinate instrumentation with Developers, work with QA/Testing to verify event and data quality, and explain outcomes and caveats to Stakeholders.
